📖 How to Order Quick guide
  1. Find your items. Search the Database or choose a ready-made Collection. Pick the color or variation you want when available.
  2. Fill your pocket. Tap + Add item to add items immediately. You can choose up to 40 item slots. Materials that support stacks show their stack sizes.
  3. Optional villager. Open Villagers and choose one if you have an open plot on your island.
  4. Review your order. Tap View order & copy $order. Check each item, adjust quantities, and choose how to fill any remaining slots.
  5. Send the command. Tap Copy $order, then paste it into the designated WishPort Discord order channel. Follow the bot's instructions for pickup.

Before you order: Not every variation or DIY recipe card is verified for bot use. Preview-only items cannot be ordered; some recipes need testing. Construction and other non-transferable items are excluded. Please use the bot's rejection message if an item is unsafe.
