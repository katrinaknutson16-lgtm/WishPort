💜 Welcome to WishPort!

Find your favorite ACNH items, build a pocket of up to 40 items, and copy your order to Discord.

📖 How to Order Quick guide
  1. Find your items. Search the Database or choose a ready-made Collection. Pick the color or variation you want when available.
  2. Fill your pocket. Tap + Add item to add items immediately. You can choose up to 40 item slots. Materials that support stacks show their stack sizes.
  3. Optional villager. Open Villagers and choose one if you have an open plot on your island.
  4. Check your order. Tap View Order to check your items, quantities, and optional filler choice.
  5. Send the command. Tap Copy order command, then paste it into the designated WishPort Discord order channel. Follow the bot's instructions for pickup.

Safety reminder: Some items and DIYs are unavailable. If WishPort or the bot flags an item, do not bypass the warning. See Help & FAQ for details.

❓ Help, FAQ & Report a Problem Quick answers and issue reporting

💜 WishPort help & FAQ

How many items can I order?

Each order holds up to 40 pocket slots. Materials can come in full stacks when a stack size is shown. One stack takes one pocket slot.

Can I adopt a villager with my items?

You can request one villager alongside your items. You must have an open plot and the bot must support villager injection. Selecting a villager does not guarantee adoption.

What happens if I don't select all 40 slots?

Choose the default bot behavior or select Nook Miles Tickets, Royal Crowns, or 99,000 Bells as filler. Open View Order to choose, then tap Copy order command.

How do I place my order?

Choose your items or a Collection, tap View Order, then tap Copy order command. Paste it into WishPort's designated Discord order channel and follow the bot's response.

What if the bot rejects my order?

Remove the item named in the error and try again. Never bypass unsafe-item warnings. You can report the rejected item below so the catalog can be checked.

Are the displayed colors and DIY cards always guaranteed?

No. Some variations require additional metadata, and certain DIY cards are unavailable or unverified. An image preview is not proof that a code is safe or that a particular variation will be delivered.

What are the Treasure Island pickup rules?

Wait for your private Dodo Code, arrive with empty pockets, collect your requested items only, don't explore, never share your Dodo Code, and leave through the airport. Have an open plot for a villager adoption.

Why are some items preview-only or blocked?

Some customization variations require additional code metadata, and special DIY recipe unlocks or non-transferable assets cannot be safely ordered. WishPort screens these items, but bot validation is the final check. Never disable or bypass the bot’s safety protections.

What about exact colors, recipe cards, and image sources?

Basic four-character item IDs may not guarantee a chosen color or pattern. Recipe cards can require encoded metadata. Exact customization may require a verified NHI inventory. Catalog pictures use the ACNH community data spreadsheet; pictures do not prove orderability.

Where are saved orders stored?

Saved orders stay in your current browser on the current device. They do not sync between phones or Discord accounts. Clearing browser storage can remove them.

✈️ What happens after I order?
  1. Check your items and tap Copy order command.
  2. Paste it into the correct WishPort Discord order channel.
  3. Wait for the bot to accept your order and send your private Dodo Code when it is your turn. A received command is not a confirmed booking or delivery.
  4. Arrive with empty pockets and enough inventory space for your items. Have an open housing plot if adopting a villager.
  5. Collect only your order, follow the island rules, and leave through the airport. Never share the Dodo Code.

Bot replies and timing vary. If WishPort rejects a code as unsafe, do not bypass its safety checks.
